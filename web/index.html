What a complete schedule carries

Header: panel designation and location, voltage and system, main breaker or MLO, bus rating, AIC rating, fed-from and feeder information. Per circuit: number (odd down the left, even down the right, A-B-C rotation), description, load type, breaker amps and poles, wire size, connected VA per phase. Summary: connected VA/kVA/amps per phase, a balance check, demand by load type, and the printable door directory. Demand factors are your design inputs — this generator totals connected load and balance; it does not apply CEC Section 8 demand rules for you.
